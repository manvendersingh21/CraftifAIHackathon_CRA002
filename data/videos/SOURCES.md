# Video sources

All clips in this folder are **real camera footage** (no simulation, no synthetic/generated video). Each was downloaded from a free-license source, then trimmed (max 60-90 s), scaled to at most 1280 px wide, stripped of audio and re-encoded to H.264 (`libx264 -crf 23`). These are the only modifications.

Machine-readable metadata: `manifest.json`. `has_ground_truth` is `false` for every clip: the helmet labels below are a human eyeball summary from sampled frames, not per-frame annotations.

## Notes on labels

- `helmets` values: `all wearing`, `none wearing`, `mixed`, `unknown/too far`.
- Mixkit clips are professionally shot stock footage of real people (staged scenes), not candid site footage.
- `wm_abseil_tower_block.mp4` shows a charity abseil participant in a climbing helmet, not a construction worker.
- `wm_prefab_house_assembly.mp4` is a time-lapse (real footage, not real-time).
