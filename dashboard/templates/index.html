How it is measured

  • Video FPS is the measured arrival rate of annotated frames at this server (last 60 frames).
  • Inference FPS is reported by the inference process, from its own timing.
  • Compliance = (1 − unique violators / unique persons) × 100.
  • Digital twin maps each bounding box's bottom-centre to the floor plane and its height to person scale. It is a schematic projection, not a calibrated 3D position.
